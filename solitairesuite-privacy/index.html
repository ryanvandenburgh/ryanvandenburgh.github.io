Privacy Policy

For Solitaire Suite · Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ryan VanDenburgh ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile game Solitaire Suite (the "App").

The short version

The App does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no in-app purchases, and it works entirely offline.

Information we collect

None. The App does not ask for your name, email, or any other personal details, and it does not send any data to us or to third parties.

Data stored on your device

The App saves your game statistics (such as games played, games won, best time and fewest moves) and your last selected game locally on your device. This data never leaves your device, is not sent to us, and is removed when you delete the App.

Third-party services

The App contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Children's privacy

Because the App collects no personal information, it collects none from children. The App is suitable for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change too.
